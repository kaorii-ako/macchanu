# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, one site:
- **Sponsors**: corporate decision-makers evaluating ROI and team credibility. They scan fast, need to feel the team is serious.
- **Competition judges**: F1 in Schools officials who assess presentation quality as part of scoring.
- **General public**: fans, curious visitors, students inspired by the story.

## Product Purpose

Macchanu Racing is six students from Amnuay Silpa School, Thailand, competing at the F1 in Schools World Finals in Japan (June 14, 2026). This site is their pitch to the world: attract sponsors, impress judges, and tell the story of a precise, ambitious team that built everything from scratch.

Success = a sponsor clicks "Sponsor Us" or a judge walks away thinking this team belongs on the world stage.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Cinematic. Quietly elite.

Not loud. Not hype. The confidence of a team that already knows they belong there. Like a creative studio that happens to race.

References: Ssscript App, STUDIO375. Editorial weight. Typographic authority. Dark, intentional, high-craft.

## Anti-references

- Generic sports team websites (hero photo + stats + sponsor logo wall)
- Startup SaaS dark mode (gradient text, glowing cards, hero-metric dashboards)
- School project aesthetics (clip art, bright primaries, Comic Sans energy)
- F1 fan sites (chequerboard patterns, tire textures, aggressive reds)
- Glassmorphism used decoratively everywhere

## Design Principles

1. **Precision as aesthetic.** The team machines parts to 0.005mm. The design should feel equally intentional. Every margin, every typographic choice is deliberate.
2. **Story over statistics.** Six Thai students going to the World Finals is remarkable. The human narrative earns trust more than animated counters.
3. **Studio-grade craft.** References are creative agencies, not sports sites. Treat this like a portfolio that races, not a race team that has a website.
4. **Underdog made elite.** Amnuay Silpa School is not a famous institution. The design should make the team feel world-class regardless.
5. **Motion earns its place.** Speed is part of the brand, but only when motion serves the story. Never decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. No specific user needs stated. Ensure sufficient contrast for dark backgrounds with gold/teal accents. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
