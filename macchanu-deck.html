Our Identity

WHY
MACCHANU?

Macchanu is the son of Hanuman — a figure of strength, unwavering dedication, and grace in Thai culture. A hybrid creature: part monkey, part fish.

We chose the fish as our logo identity, representing fluidity. Our teal and gold palette communicates calm yet bold presence — subtle in tone, impactful in identity.

Mythological strength × Hydrodynamic innovation

Teal #19757e — Fluid precision
